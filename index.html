Three steps and one odd warning.

  1. 1Download the zip and unzip it.
  2. 2Drag Hangabout.app into Applications.
  3. 3First launch, macOS will refuse — open System Settings → Privacy & Security and hit Open Anyway. Once only.

Once only really does mean once. After this your companion asks when there is a new version, and Update does the rest — no download, no drag, no warning. Settings and reminders carry across.
