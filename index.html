Hangabout

A lucky charm that hangs from the top of your Mac screen. It sways while you work, ignores every click, and swings when you flick it.

⬇  Download for Mac

Free · Apple Silicon (M1 or later) · macOS 14 Sonoma or later

It remembers things for you

A notification disappears before you have finished reading it. Instead, the thing you asked to be reminded about is written on a card the charm is holding — still there an hour later, still there after you switch Spaces.

It tucks itself away while you type and stays still while you work. That part is deliberate: something that sways all day teaches you to ignore it, and then it cannot tell you anything.

Installing it

  1. Download the zip above and double-click it.
  2. Drag Hangabout.app into your Applications folder.
  3. Double-click it. macOS will refuse the first time — see below.

macOS will say it “cannot verify Hangabout is free of malware”.

That is not a warning about this app. macOS says it about anything that has not been through Apple's $99/year notarization, and Hangabout is a hobby project.

To open it anyway: click Done, then go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll to the bottom, and click Open Anyway. Authenticate, confirm once more, and you are done — you only do this the first time.

Once it is running

A charm appears hanging from the top-right of your screen, and a hanger icon appears in your menu bar. Everything lives in that menu — reminders, which companion is hanging there, and Quit. There is no Dock icon, by design.

  • Flick the charm with your cursor to make it swing.
  • Hold ⌥ and drag to slide it anywhere along the top of the screen.
  • To remove it: quit from the menu bar, then drag the app to the Trash. It leaves nothing behind.
