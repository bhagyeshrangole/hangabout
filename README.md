# Hangabout

A lucky charm that hangs from the top of your Mac screen. It sways while you
work, ignores every click, and swings when you flick it.

It also keeps your reminders. Instead of a notification that disappears before
you read it, the thing you asked to be reminded about is written on a card the
charm is holding — still there an hour later, still there after you switch
Spaces.

## Download

Grab the latest `Hangabout.zip` from [Releases](../../releases), unzip it, and
drag `Hangabout.app` to your Applications folder.

Requires macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later, Apple Silicon.

## First launch

macOS will say **"Apple could not verify Hangabout is free of malware."**
That is Gatekeeper telling you the app is not notarized, which costs $99/yr —
it is not a statement about the app.

To open it anyway: **right-click `Hangabout.app` → Open → Open.** You only do
this once; after that it launches normally.

## Using it

The charm appears hanging from the top-right of your screen, and a hanger icon
appears in your menu bar. Everything lives in that menu — reminders, which
companion is hanging there, and Quit.

- **Flick the charm** to make it swing.
- **⌥ and drag** to slide it anywhere along the top of the screen.
- It tucks itself away when you are typing and stays still while you work, so
  it is not another thing moving in the corner of your eye all day.
